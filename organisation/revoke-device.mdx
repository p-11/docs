title: "Revoke a device"
description: "Stop an iPhone from approving requests, for example when it's lost or replaced."
---

<Info>Requires the Owner or Admin role. Revoking takes effect right away, with no approval.</Info>

## Revoking a device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member">
    Go to **Team** and click the member. Their enrolled devices are listed under **Enrolled devices**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revoke the device">
    Click **…** next to the device, then **Revoke device**. Check the device name, then click **Revoke**.

    The device can no longer approve requests for the member. To keep approving, the member needs a new device enrollment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enroll a replacement">
    [Send a device enrollment](/organisation/send-device-enrollment) so the member can enroll a new iPhone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the member holds a root key share

The revoke dialog warns you that the member holds a root key share. Their share is lost with the device, so:

1. Revoke the device. The console asks you to request a device enrollment for the member. Click **Request device enrollment**, or **Later** to do it from **Team**.
2. Once the member enrolls their new iPhone and it's approved, Strongpoint reshares the root key. Every holder gets a new share and accepts it on their iPhone.

Your root key keeps working while you do this, as long as enough holders still have their shares. If too few holders are left to approve, you need to [recover the root key](/peak/recover-root-key) from the backup key instead. The dialog tells you when that's the case.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This is your only active regular device." | Enroll another iPhone before you revoke this one, or you won't be able to log in or approve. |
| "This is their only active regular device and they are an approver on an approval policy." | Remove the member from those policies first. See [Change a policy](/policies-and-approvals/change-policy). |
| "The server denied this action." | Your role can't revoke devices. Ask an Owner or Admin. |

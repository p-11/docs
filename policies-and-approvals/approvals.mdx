Approvals are how Strongpoint authorizes actions. Before Strongpoint moves funds, invites a user, or changes a key or a policy, the right people in your organization approve it on their iPhones.

Your [policies](/policies-and-approvals/policies) decide who those people are and how many of them must approve each action. Together, policies and approvals control how funds leave your custody accounts.

## How a request moves

1. **Someone starts a request.** For example, to send funds, invite a user or change a policy. Strongpoint uses the policy for that action to set who can approve and how many approvals it needs.
2. **Approvers get a notification on their iPhone.** The request also appears on the **Approvals** page in the console.
3. **Approvers decide in the Strongpoint Authenticator.** Approvals are made only on an enrolled iPhone.
4. **Strongpoint runs or stops the action.** It runs once enough approvers approve. It stops if too many reject, or if the request expires first.

Approvals are how Strongpoint authorizes actions. Before Strongpoint moves funds, invites a user, or changes a key or a policy, the right people in your organization approve it on their iPhones. Your [policies](/policies-and-approvals/policies) decide who those people are and how many of them must approve each action.

## Request lifecycle

1. Someone starts a request, for example to send funds, invite a user or change a policy. Strongpoint uses the policy for that action to set who can approve and how many approvals it needs.
2. Approvers get a notification on their iPhone, and the request appears on the **Approvals** page in the console.
3. Approvers approve or reject the request in the Strongpoint Authenticator on their enrolled iPhone.
4. Strongpoint runs the action once enough approvers approve. It stops the action if too many reject, or if the request expires first.

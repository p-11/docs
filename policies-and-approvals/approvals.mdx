You approve sensitive actions on your iPhone before Strongpoint runs them. Inviting a user, changing a role, adding a key and changing an approval rule each start an approval request.

## How a request moves

1. **Someone starts an action.** Strongpoint finds the [approval rule](/policies-and-approvals/policies) for that action and records who can approve it and how many approvals it needs.
2. **Approvers get a notification.** It reads "Approval required" and shows no details. It goes to each approver's most recently enrolled iPhone, and the request also appears on the **Approvals** page in the console.
3. **Approvers decide on their iPhone.** Each approver reviews the request in the Strongpoint Authenticator, then approves or rejects it with Face ID or Touch ID.
4. **Strongpoint runs the action.** When enough approvals arrive, the request shows **Completed** and the action runs.

For how long a request stays open, see [Request statuses](/policies-and-approvals/request-statuses#time-windows).

## What an approver signs

Your iPhone signs a summary of what you reviewed: the action and its details, the request, its expiry time, your decision, and the time you made it. A signature made for one request cannot be reused for another. A rejection reason is shown to other approvers but is not part of the signature.

Each decision carries two signatures over that summary:

- **ECDSA (P-256).** A classical signature scheme.
- **ML-DSA-87.** A post-quantum signature scheme that NIST standardized as FIPS 204.

Both signing keys are created in your iPhone's Secure Enclave and need Face ID or Touch ID to use. Strongpoint accepts a decision only when both signatures verify.

## Approving and accepting your share

{/* TK: add iPhone screenshots of each step (the approval request, Waiting for the other holders, and Root key saved on this iPhone). They need captures from the Strongpoint Authenticator app. */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approve the request">
    Open the Strongpoint Authenticator. The request is titled "Accept your root key share", or "Accept your backup key share" on a backup iPhone. Check the organization and how many holders must approve, then tap **Approve** and confirm with Face ID or Touch ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the other holders">
    Strongpoint creates the key only after every holder approves, so the app shows "Waiting for the other holders." until the last one does. You don't need to keep the app open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your share">
    When the console shows "Open app to accept your root key", open the Strongpoint Authenticator and unlock it. Confirm with Face ID or Touch ID to retrieve your share. The app shows "Root key saved on this iPhone."
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a backup iPhone, power it off after you accept your share, and store it in a safe place.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This root key request has expired or was already handled." | Ask your Owner to request the root key again. |
| "This request was replaced by a newer one. Ask your admin to start again." | Approve the newer request instead. |
| "Root key saved. Restart to confirm." | Close and reopen the Strongpoint Authenticator. |
| "Root key not saved. Ask your admin." | Contact your Owner or Admin. |

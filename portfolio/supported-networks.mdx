title: "Supported networks"
description: "The networks and assets Strongpoint custody supports, with their confirmations and fees."
---

| Network | Asset | Addresses | Confirmations for a deposit | Network fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zcash | ZEC | Transparent (starting with `t1`) | 10 | Set by Strongpoint under the Zcash fee rules (ZIP 317). From 0.0001 ZEC, more for larger transactions |

Each Zcash wallet has one transparent address, and shows the **Transparent** label.

The console lists more assets when you create an account or add a wallet. Choose the networks in the table above to receive and send funds.

## Block explorer

To look up a Zcash transaction, open it on the account's **Activity** tab and click **Open in explorer**.

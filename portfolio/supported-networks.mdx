| Network | Asset | Addresses | Confirmations for a deposit | Network fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zcash | ZEC | Transparent (starting with `t1`) | 10 (about 13 minutes) | Set by Strongpoint under the Zcash fee rules |

Each Zcash wallet has one transparent address, and shows the **Transparent** label.

The console lists more assets when you create an account or add a wallet. Choose the networks in the table above to receive and send funds.

## Block explorers

To look up a transaction or address, use the recommended explorer for its network. The console's **Open in explorer** button opens the same one.

| Network | Recommended explorer |
|---|---|
| Zcash | [Zcash Explorer](https://mainnet.zcashexplorer.app) |

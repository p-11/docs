The Strongpoint Authenticator is the iPhone app you use to enroll a device, approve every console sign-in, and approve sensitive operations.

You can install it before you open your invitation, or when onboarding asks you to. Enrollment, log-in, and approvals all need this app on an iPhone.

## Before you start

- You need an iPhone. Strongpoint enrollment is built for iPhone; open enrollment links on your iPhone, not on another phone.
- You need access to the App Store on that iPhone.
- [TK: confirm the public App Store listing URL before linking it here.]

## How to download the app

### During onboarding

1. Reach the **Download the Strongpoint app** step in the console after you set up your identity.
2. If the console shows a download QR code, scan it with your iPhone Camera or App Store flow and install **Strongpoint**.
3. If the download QR is unavailable, install Strongpoint from the App Store on your iPhone, then return to the console.
4. Tap **I have the app** when the app is installed.

### Before you open your invitation

1. On your iPhone, open the App Store.
2. Search for **Strongpoint** and install the Strongpoint Authenticator. [TK: replace with the official App Store link when the listing URL is confirmed.]
3. Open the app once so it is ready when you enroll.

In the console this step still says **Strongpoint app**. In these docs we call it the **Strongpoint Authenticator**, which matches how Approvals describes the same app.

## Limits and common mistakes

- There is no Android enrollment path in the product. Use an iPhone.
- Do not invent or share an App Store URL from memory. Prefer the QR the console shows when it is configured, or the official listing your Strongpoint contact provides.
- Installing the app alone does not enroll you. You still need to accept your invitation and complete [Enroll your iPhone](/onboarding/enroll-iphone).

## If something goes wrong

- **The console says the download QR is unavailable.** Install Strongpoint from the App Store on your iPhone, then tap **I have the app**.
- **You cannot find the app.** Contact Support or your organisation's Owner for the current listing details. [TK: App Store availability.]
- **You already installed the app.** Tap **I have the app** and continue. You do not need to reinstall.

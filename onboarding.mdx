To join Strongpoint, you need an invitation from someone on your team. If you are the first user, Project Eleven invites you.

You need a computer and an iPhone. After setup, you confirm every login and every approval on that iPhone, so use one you keep with you.

## Every user

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="1. Accept your invitation" icon="envelope-open" href="/onboarding/accept-invite">
    Open the email link and choose your login method: passkey or Google.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Enroll your iPhone" icon="mobile-screen-button" href="/onboarding/enroll-iphone">
    Link the Strongpoint Authenticator to your login.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Log in to the console" icon="right-to-bracket" href="/onboarding/log-in">
    Go to Strongpoint, log in, and confirm on your iPhone.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

As an approver, you then review requests on your iPhone. See [Approve or reject a request](/policies-and-approvals/approve-request).

## Owners and Admins

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="4. Invite your team" icon="user-plus" href="/onboarding/invite-users">
    Each teammate needs their own invitation, and approvers must approve it before Strongpoint sends it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="5. Create an account" icon="wallet" href="/onboarding/create-account">
    Accounts hold your assets and set who approves payments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send a device enrollment" icon="mobile-retro" href="/organisation/send-device-enrollment">
    Set up a new iPhone for a member who replaced theirs.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To bring a new organization to Strongpoint, contact [Strongpoint support](mailto:support@strongpoint.projecteleven.com).

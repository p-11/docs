Strongpoint has no sign-up page. You join when someone in your organization invites you by email.

Your iPhone is central to how Strongpoint works. Once you are set up, Strongpoint asks your iPhone to confirm every login and every approval. Enroll an iPhone that you keep with you.

Set aside one sitting at your computer, with your iPhone beside you.

## Every user

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="1. Accept your invitation" icon="envelope-open" href="/onboarding/accept-invite">
    Open the email link and choose a passkey or Google. You use it to identify yourself every time you log in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Enroll your iPhone" icon="mobile-screen-button" href="/onboarding/enroll-iphone">
    Link the Strongpoint Authenticator to your login. Without it, you cannot log in or approve anything.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Log in to the console" icon="right-to-bracket" href="/onboarding/log-in">
    Log in on your computer, then confirm on your iPhone. You do this every time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Owners and Admins

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="4. Invite your team" icon="user-plus" href="/onboarding/invite-users">
    Each teammate needs their own invitation, and approvers must approve it before Strongpoint sends it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="5. Create an account" icon="wallet" href="/onboarding/create-account">
    Accounts hold your assets and set who approves changes and payments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

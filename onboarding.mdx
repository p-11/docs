Onboarding walks you from invitation to a working console session, then to inviting teammates and creating a custody account.

You join Strongpoint when your organisation invites you. After you enroll an iPhone with the Strongpoint Authenticator, every console sign-in and every sensitive operation is approved on that device. Organisation creation is handled by Strongpoint for your team; you do not create an organisation in the console yourself.

## Steps

1. **[Download the Strongpoint Authenticator](/onboarding/download-app)**  
   Install the iPhone app you will use for enrollment, log-in approval, and operation approvals.

2. **[Accept your invitation](/onboarding/accept-invite)**  
   Open the email link, confirm your invite details and name, and continue onboarding in the console.

3. **[Set up your identity](/onboarding/set-up-identity)**  
   Create a passkey or link Google, depending on your organisation, so you can sign in later.

4. **[Enroll your iPhone](/onboarding/enroll-iphone)**  
   Scan the enrollment QR code, confirm the six-digit code, and wait if an approver must approve the device.

5. **[Log in to the console](/onboarding/log-in)**  
   Sign in at the dashboard. Every console sign-in is approved on your iPhone.

6. **[Invite your team](/onboarding/invite-users)**  
   Owners and Admins request invitations from Team. Strongpoint emails the invitation only after the request is approved.

7. **[Create an account](/onboarding/create-account)**  
   After you can log in, add a custody account in Portfolio, set its approval rules, then add wallets as you need them.

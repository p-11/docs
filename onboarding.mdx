Strongpoint requires an invitation from someone on your team. If you are the first user, Project Eleven invites you.

Setup takes your computer and your iPhone. After setup, you confirm every login and every approval on that iPhone, so use one you keep with you.

## Every user

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="1. Accept your invitation" icon="envelope-open" href="/onboarding/accept-invite">
    Open the email link and choose your login method: passkey or Google.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Enroll your iPhone" icon="mobile-screen-button" href="/onboarding/enroll-iphone">
    Link the Strongpoint Authenticator to your login identity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Log in to the console" icon="right-to-bracket" href="/onboarding/log-in">
    Go to Strongpoint, log in, and confirm on your iPhone.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Owners and Admins

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="4. Invite your team" icon="user-plus" href="/onboarding/invite-users">
    Each teammate needs their own invitation, and approvers must approve it before Strongpoint sends it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="5. Create an account" icon="wallet" href="/onboarding/create-account">
    Accounts hold your assets and set who approves changes and payments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
